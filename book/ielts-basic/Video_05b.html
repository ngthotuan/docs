Sửa bài tập: Chủ động & Bị động

Video 05b - IELTS Grammar Notes

💡 Key Takeaways & Grammar Rules

1. Cấu trúc cơ bản của Câu bị động

Công thức chung: S + To be + V3/ed + (by O).

Lỗi sai phổ biến nhất là thiếu động từ to be hoặc chia sai dạng V3/ed.

  • Động từ khiếm khuyết (Modal Verbs): Modal + be + V3/ed (VD: can't be explained, should be returned, might be thrown).
  • Giới từ (Prepositions): Sau giới từ như before, after, động từ phải ở dạng V-ing. Do đó dạng bị động sẽ là before/after + being + V3/ed (VD: after being arrested, before being put).
  • Động từ Need: Bị động của need là need to be V3/ed (VD: needs to be tackled).
  • Vị trí trạng từ (Adverbs): Trạng từ thường được đặt giữa động từ to be và V3/ed (VD: is seriously polluted, was spelt correctly).
  • Cụm từ chỉ thời gian: Các cụm từ như When I was young là dấu hiệu của thì Quá khứ đơn, nên động từ ở vế trước hoặc sau đó cũng phải chia ở Quá khứ đơn (VD: I learned to swim when I was young).

📝 Exercise 1: Error Correction

Phát hiện và sửa lỗi sai trong các câu sau:

1. These waste glass bottles sorted by colour and washed by high-pressure water.
→ are sorted
Thiếu động từ "to be" (are) cho thì hiện tại đơn.
2. Any impurities are remove.
→ removed
Động từ chính phải ở dạng V3/ed.
3. With this kind of law, people have no choice and forced to use buses and trains.
→ are forced
Thiếu động từ "to be" (are).
4. Athens only was allowed a certain number of cars into the city centre in the past.
→ allowed
Dùng sai thể. Câu này mang nghĩa chủ động (Athens cho phép...), không phải bị động.
5. Nowadays, air pollution is affected us all.
→ affects
Dùng sai thể. Ô nhiễm không khí ảnh hưởng đến chúng ta (chủ động).
6. This area is surround by water.
→ surrounded
Động từ chính phải ở dạng V3/ed.
7. The flowchart shows how banana chips made.
→ are made
Thiếu động từ "to be" (are).
8. The way we live has affected in a big way by things like the Internet...
→ has been affected
Câu bị động thì Hiện tại hoàn thành: have/has + been + V3/ed.
9. A mystery is something that can't explained.
→ be explained
Bị động với Modal verb: can + be + V3/ed.
10. If I didn't do my job properly, I would be sack.
→ sacked
Động từ chính phải ở dạng V3/ed.
11. I haven't received the letter. It might be send to the wrong address.
→ sent
Động từ chính phải ở dạng V3 (send -> sent).
12. All travel expenses for this training course must be pay by employers.
→ paid
Động từ chính phải ở dạng V3 (pay -> paid).

🔄 Exercise 2: Active to Passive

Chuyển các câu chủ động sau sang bị động:

1. They take the fruit to the cannery in large trucks.
The fruit is taken to the cannery in large trucks.
2. You should return completed questionnaires to this address.
Completed questionnaires should be returned to this address.
3. They check the fruit before storage and they reject any poor quality fruit.
The fruit is checked before storage and any poor quality fruit is rejected.
4. They advise you to return the application form within three days.
You are advised to return the application form within three days.
5. Schools should not allow fast food restaurants to sell their products to students.
Fast food restaurants should not be allowed to sell their products to students.
6. They place the chemicals in the beaker.
The chemicals are placed in the beaker.
7. They made these regulations to protect children.
These regulations were made to protect children.
8. John spelt that word correctly.
That word was spelt correctly.
9. Police used tear gas to disperse the crowds.
Tear gas was used to disperse the crowds.
10. They used the building as a warehouse.
The building was used as a warehouse.
11. People often use 'Ethics' and 'morality' interchangeably.
'Ethics' and 'morality' are often used interchangeably.
12. They do not allow candidates to use dictionaries in the exam.
Candidates are not allowed to use dictionaries in the exam.
13. They sometimes use the church as a concert venue.
The church is sometimes used as a concert venue.
14. They have repaired the damage to the ship.
The damage to the ship has been repaired.
15. We had to return the hairdryer to the store because it was faulty.
The hairdryer had to be returned to the store because it was faulty.
16. They make wine from grapes.
Wine is made from grapes.
17. He wrote this book in 1990 and publish it one year later.
This book was written in 1990 and published one year later.
18. They collect used glass from different sources.
Used glass is collected from different sources.
19. They recycle these materials into other packaging products.
These materials are recycled into other packaging products.
20. They can't make tea with cold water.
Tea can't be made with cold water.

✍️ Exercise 3: Translation & Sentence Building

Dịch câu hoặc hoàn thành câu với từ cho sẵn:

1. Đầu tiên, đất sét được đào lên khỏi mặt đất.
First, clay is dug out of the ground.
2. Sau đó, nó được trộn với cát và nước trước khi được đặt vào 1 cái khuôn để tạo ra gạch.
After that, it is mixed with sand and water before being put in a mould to make bricks.
3. Căn nhà được sơn ngày hôm qua.
The house was painted yesterday.
4. Chiếc máy tính đã bị đánh cắp cách đây vài ngày.
The computer/laptop was stolen several days ago.
5. Những chiếc máy này được sử dụng để cắt gạch.
These machines are used to cut bricks.
6. Mọi người nên được khuyến khích sử dụng phương tiện công cộng.
People should be encouraged to use public transport.
7. Sô cô la được làm từ hạt cocoa.
Chocolate is made from cocoa beans.
8. Những địa điểm công nghiệp mới sẽ được xây dựng xa trung tâm thành thị lớn.
New industrial sites will be built away from large urban centres.
9. Vấn đề này cần được giải quyết ngay lập tức.
This problem needs to be tackled immediately.
10. Hàng năm, một diện tích rừng lớn đã bị chặt cho những mục đích khác nhau.
Every year, a large area of forests is cut down for different purposes.
11. Ở các thành phố lớn, không khí bị ô nhiễm một cách nghiêm trọng.
In big/major cities, the air is seriously polluted.
12. Những cái chai này sẽ được vận chuyển tới nhà bạn bằng 1 chiếc xe tải lớn.
These bottles will be transported to your house by a big truck.
13. The concert/ hold/ at the university/ next week.
The concert will be held at the university next week.
14. As a child/ I/ surround/ by love and kindness.
As a child, I was surrounded by love and kindness.
15. A few years ago/ the floods/ cause/ heavy rainfall in the mountains.
A few years ago, the floods were caused by heavy rainfall in the mountains.
16. Last year/ the ship/ blow/ onto the rocks.
Last year, the ship was blown onto the rocks.
17. Last year/ samples/ collect/ from over 200 patients.
Last year, samples were collected from over 200 patients.
18. The car/ steal/ 1999.
The car was stolen in 1999.
19. Nowadays/ these examination papers/ score/ by machine.
Nowadays, these examination papers are scored by machine.
20. Yesterday/ after/ arrest/ he/ take/ the police station.
Yesterday after being arrested, he was taken to the police station.
21. I/ leave/ some papers/ on the desk/ last night/ and I think/ they/ might/ throw/ away.
I left some papers on the desk last night and I think they might be thrown away.
22. I/ learn/ swim/ when/ I/ very young/ and I/ teach/ by my mother.
I learned to swim when I was very young and I was taught by my mother.
